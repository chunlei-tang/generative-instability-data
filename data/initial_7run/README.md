# Initial 7-Run Experiments

This folder documents the initial experimental phase of the study.

## Models

- ChatGPT 5.1 Thinking
- Gemini 3 Pro

## Repeated Runs

Each item was evaluated across 7 repeated runs.

## Experimental Conditions

The initial experiments included:

- E1: conflicting evidence condition
- E2: standardized comparison condition

These experiments provided the initial characterization of decision instability across hierarchical decision levels.

## Role in the Revised Manuscript

The initial 7-run experiments are retained for descriptive comparison with the revised experiments.

The primary statistical analyses reported in the revised manuscript are based on the revised 20-run experiments using newer model versions.

Because the two experimental phases differed in model versions, number of repeated runs, and execution protocol, comparisons across phases should be interpreted descriptively rather than as causal estimates of model improvement.
